Roblox UGC trend research: sample of the deliverable

Two of the six ideas, worked out in full in the exact format the final sheet will use. Researched 18 Nov 2026 from public trend sources.

How each idea is chosen

  1. Signal: a dance, meme or look that several unrelated creators copied in the last 30 days (not one lucky video).
  2. Evidence: a working source link and the public numbers on it (views, plays, likes) with the date they were read.
  3. Fit: can it be read in 2 to 4 seconds on a blocky avatar? Is the move or item recognisable without the audio?
  4. Gap: a check of the Roblox Marketplace for an existing item with the same idea before it goes on the list.
  5. Format: emote, animation pack or accessory, with the reason that format suits it.

Idea 1: "Spin Trio" emote (the Chipmunks Dance)

EmoteGroup-friendlyPeaked Oct, still spreading Nov

The trendThree people stand still, burst out on the beat, take turns rushing the camera, spin and swap places, to a sped-up "You Spin Me Round (Like a Record)".
Evidence (read 18 Nov 2026)Most-played version about 20.6M plays (sibling trio, 17 Oct); first version past 10M plays at 14.7M (college trio, 10 Oct); further versions at 9.5M, 7.7M and 6.5M plays. Listed as trend no. 4 in a weekly TikTok trend roundup updated 16 Nov 2026.
ConceptA 6-second looping emote: freeze pose, bounce forward, quarter turn, step back, finishing spin. Built so three friends who fire it at once look like the trio in the videos.
Why it can sellThe trend is about doing it with friends, which is exactly how players use emotes in hangout games. The moves are big, cartoonish and readable on any avatar body. Short choreography means a clean loop.
RiskDo not use the song or the cartoon characters: name and animate the moves only.
Fit score9/10

Idea 2: "Shoulder Bounce" emote (Dame Hombro)

EmoteSolo or groupRising since Oct

The trendA loose, confident shoulder bounce on the beat of Bulin 47 & Kevin Wai's "Dame Hombro", with room for each person's own style.
Evidence (read 18 Nov 2026)The song was released 7 Oct 2026; one month later it had more than 77 million views and more than 700,000 likes on YouTube, per a news report on the dance going viral. Listed as trend no. 8 in the same weekly roundup (16 Nov 2026).
ConceptA 4-second loop: shoulder bounce on the beat, slight lean, head nod, one arm swing at the end of the loop. Simple enough to look good on R6 and R15 bodies.
Why it can sellOne move, instantly recognisable, works solo and in groups, and fits idle and "vibe" moments in social games. Strong Spanish-language reach, which many emotes ignore.
RiskSimple moves get copied fast: speed to market matters more than polish.
Fit score8/10
